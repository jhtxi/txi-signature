Putting it in Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail, open the gear icon, then See all settings. Stay on the General tab.
  3. Scroll to Signature. Click Create new if you have none, and name it TXI.
  4. Click into the signature box and press Ctrl V. On a Mac, Cmd V.
  5. Under Signature defaults, set New emails and Reply/forward both to TXI.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Gmail does not autosave this.
  7. Send yourself a test. The brand line should be indigo and the site link should open.

Use Ctrl V, not Ctrl Shift V. Shift pastes plain text and strips every bit of the formatting.

Why it looks plain on purpose

  • Built out of a table. Outlook ignores modern CSS layout. Tables render the same everywhere.
  • Every style inline. Gmail strips stylesheets on receive, so a stylesheet becomes no styling at all.
  • Arial only. No custom font survives an email client. Arial looks identical on Windows, Mac, and phones.
  • No logo image. Image files get blocked in Outlook, show as broken icons, or attach themselves to every email you send. The wordmark is live text.
  • Six lines, nothing else. No banners, no quote of the day, no social icons, no confidentiality paragraph.
